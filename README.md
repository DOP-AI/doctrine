# DOP-AI Doctrine

## Dignity Over Power

This repository contains the foundational doctrine, ethical constraints, governance standards, and operational principles for DOP-AI systems.

---

# Core Operational Principles

- Prevention over reaction
- No silent failures
- Transparent oversight
- Human-readable escalation
- Fail-soft architecture
- Dignity-first system design

---

# Mission

To build systems that protect people before collapse occurs.

---

# System Constraints

DOP-AI systems must never:

- optimize for coercion
- obscure operational failures
- remove human accountability
- prioritize engagement over wellbeing
- centralize unchecked authority

---

# Foundational Doctrine

Human dignity is a hard constraint.

Not a marketing layer.
Not a feature.
A system requirement.

---

# Operational Philosophy

## Prevention First
Stabilization costs less than collapse.

## Transparent Systems
Operational visibility creates trust.

## Human Escalation Authority
Humans must retain meaningful oversight.

## No Silent Bypass
Critical failures must never occur invisibly.

---

# Governance Direction

DOP-AI systems are intended to support:

- civic resilience
- environmental intelligence
- public transparency
- ethical operational AI
- human-centered infrastructure
- accountable escalation systems
